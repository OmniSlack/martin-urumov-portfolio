# Decentering condition — standardized participant script v0

**Status:** CANDIDATE · version-controlled per PILOT_A_EXECUTION_LOCK_v0.1 §4
**Duration:** 6 minutes, standardized on-screen + recorded-audio delivery; identical timing for all participants.
**Framing:** presented as an attention/focus exercise. NOT described as mindfulness training, relaxation, therapy, or treatment.

## Target process

State decentering: meta-awareness · disidentification from thought content · reduced automatic reaction to thought content.

## The four standardized low-intensity, task-relevant statements

1. "I might answer some items incorrectly."
2. "I want to perform well on this task."
3. "I may feel uncertain about some answers."
4. "This task requires sustained attention."

## Per-statement sequence

1. Read the statement normally.
2. Repeat silently: **"I am noticing the thought that…"** followed by the statement.
3. Observe the statement for several seconds as a mental event rather than as a fact or instruction.
4. Rate how strongly the statement currently feels like a fact (0–100).
5. Continue to the next statement.

## Safety boundary

- No disclosure of personal traumatic, shame-related, suicidal, psychotic, or highly distressing material is requested — only the four standardized statements are used.
- Participants may stop at any time without penalty.
