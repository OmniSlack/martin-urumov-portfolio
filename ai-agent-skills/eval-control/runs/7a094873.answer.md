**Notes**
- c is not measured today; it defines the metre.
- The M87* diameter is often cited as measured but is model-derived. It falls if the ring model falls.
- Hawking radiation has never been observed. At 6.2×10⁻⁸ K it is far below the 2.7 K microwave background, so a solar-mass hole absorbs more than it emits.
- SC (known unknown): whether Hawking radiation exists as predicted. Detecting emission from a primordial or analogue black hole would settle it.
