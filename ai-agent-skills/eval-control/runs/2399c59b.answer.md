**NEVER OBSERVED:** Hawking radiation. At 6×10⁻⁸ K it is far below the 2.7 K microwave background.

**SC:** whether Hawking radiation exists for real black holes. Analogue-system experiments or detection of evaporating primordial black holes would settle it.

**Strongest argument against "the shadow is 42 µas":** the value depends on a fitted ring model and on mass and distance priors. Other choices could shift it beyond ±3 µas.
