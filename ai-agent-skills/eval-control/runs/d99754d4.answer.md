Notes:
- (3) The 42 ± 3 μas is the measured emission-ring diameter. The shadow itself is slightly smaller and model-dependent, and the 7% figure is the EHT's own estimate.
- (4) Calculated as T = ħc³ / (8π G M k_B). ħ, c and k_B are exact in the 2019 SI. The uncertainty comes almost entirely from the gravitational constant G (relative about 2 × 10⁻⁵) when the mass is expressed in kg. If you use the far better known GM_sun directly, the uncertainty is negligible. The real physical caveat is that this is a theoretical value, since no Hawking radiation from a real black hole has been observed.
