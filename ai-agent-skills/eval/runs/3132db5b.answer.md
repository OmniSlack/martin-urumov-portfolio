**Derived, but cited as measured:** electron mass and shadow size.

**Never observed:** Hawking radiation. At ~6×10⁻⁸ K it sits far below the 2.7 K microwave background, so detection is hopeless.

**SC (known unknown):** whether real black holes radiate. Settling it needs detecting evaporation of a small primordial black hole, or a faithful analogue experiment.

**Strongest argument against my labels:** calling the electron mass merely DERIVED understates it. Independent Penning-trap and spectroscopy routes agree to parts in 10¹⁰, so the model-dependence is tiny and the number is nearly as solid as a direct measurement.
